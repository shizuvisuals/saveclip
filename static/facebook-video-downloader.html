How to download Facebook videos

Download public Facebook videos, including posts from pages and public profiles. Paste the video link above and choose how much quality you need.

  1. Copy the link. On a video, tap Share, then Copy link. Links from fb.watch also work.
  2. Paste it into the box above.
  3. Tap Regular, HD or AI HD and wait for the download to start.

FAQ

Why does my Facebook link fail?

Videos posted to groups or shared only with friends are private and can't be downloaded. Try a public video.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no signup and no limit on how many times you can visit, though downloads per hour are capped to keep the service fast for everyone.

Which quality should I pick?

Regular is the fastest and smallest. HD gives you up to 1080p when the original is available. AI HD upscales and sharpens the video, so it takes longer but can look better on low-resolution clips.
